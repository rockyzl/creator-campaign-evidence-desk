How this demo works Sources, tools & limits

The data

Public NeoReach campaign pages provide the brief and selected video links. YouTube oEmbed returns public title and publisher metadata. The first screen is a dated research snapshot; a live check is labeled separately.

The agent

Vertex AI chooses read-only tools in a LangGraph loop. SQLite keeps the campaign and content records aligned. Citation IDs and a separate model assessment check the draft before your review.

Your review

You can approve the report or mark it for follow-up. This is a demo reviewer role, scoped to this browser. Reviews are saved on the server. No email is sent and no real campaign system is changed.

We have no contracts, deadlines, payments, sales or private analytics. No video playback or content-quality verification is performed. No affiliation with NeoReach or NVIDIA.
